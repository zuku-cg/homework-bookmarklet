A clearer homework list for DPR

A bookmark that redraws your DPR homework as one simple list: what's late, what's due this week, and which evening you plan to do each piece.

On a computer

Use Chrome or Safari. The bookmark needs a bookmarks bar, and some browsers (Dia and Arc, for example) don't have one.

  1. Show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B, or Cmd+Shift+B on a Mac).
  2. Drag this button onto the bar: Homework
  3. Log in to DPR as usual, then click the bookmark. Click it again to go back to normal DPR.

On an iPad or phone

Use Safari on an iPad or iPhone, or Chrome on Android.

  1. Tap Copy the bookmark code below.
  2. Bookmark this page (Share, then Add Bookmark) and call it Homework.
  3. Open your bookmarks, edit that bookmark, delete its address and paste the code in its place. Save.
  4. Log in to DPR, then open your bookmarks and tap Homework.

Copied. Now follow steps 2 to 4.

What it does and doesn't do

  • It only works while you're logged in to DPR yourself. It never sees your password.
  • It sends nothing anywhere except to DPR. There is no server, no account and no tracking.
  • Ticking something off ticks it on DPR too, the same as ticking the card there, so your teacher may see it. It never hands work in. Work set as Submit Online still has to be handed in on DPR.
  • Your evening plans are saved only in your own browser.
  • It isn't made by DPR or your school. If DPR changes, it may stop working until it's updated.
